Data Management

Your data is stored on YOUR computer. Export, backup, and restore anytime.

Set a folder path (e.g. E:\MyBackups or D:\POS-Backup). Your data will be backed up here automatically every day.

Download your data as CSV (opens in Excel) or as a full database backup file.

📦 Export Products (CSV) 💰 Export Sales (CSV) 💾 Download Full Backup (.db)

Upload a CSV file with columns: barcode, name, price, cost, category, stock, taxable, ebt_eligible, age_restricted

If you move to a new PC or recover from a crash — upload your .db backup file to restore everything.

For an extra cloud backup layer. Place your google-credentials.json file in the app folder to enable.

Receipt Printer

Works with any receipt printer you install on this PC — Epson, Star, Bixolon, or any other brand. Just install its Windows driver first (so it shows up below), then pick it here.

Printer selection is only available in the Sal POS desktop app (this looks like a browser tab).

Prints a small test receipt to confirm the selected printer actually works.

Barcode Scanner

No setup needed — any USB or Bluetooth scanner works out of the box, as long as it's in "keyboard wedge" / "HID" mode, which is the default on virtually every scanner sold.

Click the box below, then scan any barcode to confirm your scanner is working.
